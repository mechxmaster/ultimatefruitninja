How to Play

👆
Swipe to Slice
Drag your finger or mouse across flying fruits to cleave them in two!

Slice 3, 4, 5+ fruits in one single fluid stroke to earn huge combo multipliers.

Beware of Bombs!
In Classic Mode, slicing a bomb detonates and ends your game. Let bombs fall harmlessly!
